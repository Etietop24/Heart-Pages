WHISPER 14 · REFLECTION

With Flaws and All

02.09.2026 4 min read

It's been over a year and we are still on the same thing.

In my opinion, I have no absolute right to blame you. Truth is, I never got the chance to reminisce or look back at the solid short time we had. I've always been in control of my emotions that I had to suppress every flint of care.

Truthfully, these past few months and year had been on me since. Someone asked me, “If I were to be approached by you in a conversation, how would I respond?”

I realized that no matter how I thought about it, I was always angry.

Seriously, I have come to realize that when they mean “they love hard,” they do.

Words can't process these emotions I'm feeling. Because literally, I've missed you—heck, serious.

Love, it loves Flaws and All.

With all my stupid ways, my conviction, my rule of life, my voice, my scars, my marks, my pimples, my ugly face, my pride, my ego, my bewildered speech, my unguarded mouth, my life in whole—you loved me to bits.

I can't say how much I did love those things about you, but for the records, I do now love how you walk, your body, your height, your smile, your charisma, the fact that you pretend you care no shit but you do, the fact that you block me and still unblock me.

The reason you keep my messages without replying is what keeps me in a confused state.

I mean, I know you don't want anything to do with me, but I know it still hurts, deep down.

Moreover, I am telling you, it still does for a million thousand reasons why.

Four years are enough to lose my mind.

Four years, I was ignorant while you were holding.

Four years, I was chilling out, while you were heating.

Four years, I was living my life as I pleased while you were believing my heart.

Four years, I was gaining others' hearts while yours was still trapped in mine.

Four years, I was glowing with nonchalance while you were insisting on a move to make me yours.

Therefore, four years, I was ignorant of how much you loved me.

It took two weeks to break.

Two weeks weren't just fourteen days, it was not just more hours than seven days.

It wasn't just a one-word reply or a conversation, it wasn't just “I love you, goodnight.”

It was more than a thousand miles to now, it was a thousand reasons to understand the hurt now.

It was a means to remind me that Love, “It loves Flaws and All.”

With all my imperfections, my crooked ways, my bent life, you chose to mingle with me—a girl with the least popularity, a girl who believed the less in love.

Classic epic film, right? Cause I am the one watching your every move, waiting for a glance, a glimpse—even a glare would be so much helpful.

However, in all these, I can't condone it all without a conversation, one where we got to tell ourselves the damn truth.

It's okay if it still hurts, cause that's what makes it real. Whether or not we like it, we did have something, but it's gonna be painful if we go on in this next phase of our lives.

So I suggest we keep our pride down and talk it out like frigging adults that we are.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
