A Letter To My Love

04.08.2026 5 min read

Dear Love,

It sure had been a long time since I did this but this life just had me on a tight path. As I dey speak now, I know say how my life dey be but I decided to cruise back into memories to at least reminisce.

Maybe am not being direct in my statement but over these past years, I have come across the most sooth pleasing books, books that make your soul feel warm and you daydream of your brightest morning. I mean I have not always been a romantic but I think I learnt from them.

Sometimes now, feeling emotions has been harder for me. Most times, I often want to cry intentionally or blush harder than usual or get sad to feel emotions but it could explained with no words. Books are majorly where I find solace.

Sometimes, it is harder in reality to let them out. At times, all I do is just sit still and stare while the emotions keep burning harder. It is like there is a hard wall against it that was built over the years.

There are times that I love to feel the way the books I read feel like “when the girl is crushing on this cool handsome guy” and am like “this is not reality” but after everything, she got a shocking rejection cause a minor situation.

Therefore, am saying that I could have but these past years have been a close up for me as there is no one to express this too.

Am good but this emotions keep piling and building behind those hardcore walls, walls of pain, betrayal, broken trust and anger.

This walls just got to be removed before this Love they call romantic comes rushing down. Until then my love, I do not know when but soon…

You just have to wait maybe in my next life but I just do want you to know that no matter how distant I am to you, know that we still do connect.

Sometimes it’s all your face I see in everyone, the smile across their faces reminds me of a person out there rooting for me; someone who knows the Me, no one found out about.

Or should I talk about your eyes, alone are enough to make me go into my dreams even at daytime. The color drives me into illusions that do not want to give away sure it may seem silly but we both know that with you, all I say are truth continually.

They say desperate needs calls for desperate measures.

Am sure you do not want to forget how much I love your silky black hair and no doubt when the smell of your cologne slaps me hard in the face that all I wanna breathe is You.

Well, things have taken a hectic path but with me in your arm, I really relieved that the sun will still shine and the rainbow to dance into the sky.

Things may never always be rosy but I guess even the thorny ones are subject to you when you come around.

All your words are like music in my ear, each day, I try to practice them; converting them to my dance moves.

They say beauty is in the eye of the beholder but I sure do know that we only both could perceive the incredible aura we both shared.

People say couples talk all the times but we do not need the words cause our eyes speak things that others cannot read.

This might be insane but it may never get to you but hold onto this words: “IT’S ONLY YOU, no one else.”

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
