If I Had to Lose You…

WHISPER 15 · LETTER

13.09.2026 4 min read

One time, I was cruising in my shoes, the next, I was exploiting life with a stranger.

Its been a month and still I can’t make sense of who exactly am talking to all day. It was first a harmless conversation of lowering my pride, trying to reach out to someone and then it went onto a day without you won’t be the same.

It felt more like an hour with you each day would let me rest. To be honest, I hadn’t realized what you mean to me but trust me, my words mean a lot.

Have scattered my word bank, scanned the internet, even queried AI for not coming up with the perfect words to describe you. What we have has kinda turned into something that I would miss if you go.

Leaving me hanging has put a lot of thoughts in my head. I don’t want it to be an arrest, a mob, a gang attack or even another broken leg. You know I care a lot about how reckless you behave that I don’t even mind cause sometimes if it makes you feel good, then its okay for me.

Cannot term this as love but I wanna say that you mean a lot, more than my words could express.

The way you stack up my DM with messages, how you make sure my day turns out good, calls me to let me know that not replying to your messages doesn’t give you peace of mind.

Its funny how we spend an hour each day, not necessarily, talking but enjoying each other’s silence. I know sometimes we doze off on the call but that doesn’t mean I hate you to the least.

I admire your rantings and your tantrums. To be honest, hearing someone go gaga over my absence makes me feel a lot good that I found someone that is willing to give me more in exchange for nothing.

Waking up, I have thoughts of not talking to you, not that it’s a good thing but cause, I know if I get in too deep, I won’t be able to pull out.

I promised, vowed and told myself not to get in too deep. I still do stand by it. I wouldn’t lie leaving this part of me had been a lot to process.

Leaving the “Good morning…” behind

Leaving the calls at 2pm alone

Leaving the “asking about their day” question

Knowing fully well that someone wouldn’t ask me if I had eaten

Knowing that the jokes about me eating four wraps of swallow won’t be there kills me

Having to sleep with the thought of someone not dozing off on a conversation

How pulling your legs in serious moments felt like to you

Speculating about how good you sound when you are speaking Yoruba

Sincerely, if am allowed to make one big change in this huge thing they call life, I would it be that I never have to lose the one person that felt good just by hearing my voice.

To be honest, I will do anything if it means, I can allow you in my life.

I do miss you on cold nights,

Trust you on dark nights

Listen for your voice on silent nights

Long for your jokes on tense nights

Dream of you on hard nights

Moreover, still love you on Loveless nights.

I wish I never had to do this but we can’t change the world to ours even if we tried.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
