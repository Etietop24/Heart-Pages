WHISPER 04 · FRAGMENT

You Broke ME

18.08.2026 2 min read

It began with a friendly note.

It wasn’t the boisterous one,
With bold wings and confident looks.

However, more pages changed environment.
It became a Chapter.

And in that Chapter, we had a Journey.
In that Journey were Experiences.
In each Experience were Moments.
In each Moment were Decisions.
Furthermore, in each Decision were Choices.

They were out of either love or simplicity.
It was by either luck or love.
It was either a duty or an order.
It was either voluntary or compulsory.
It was either “For Love's Sake” or “From the Heart.”

And as each Choice was made,
A Decision was made.
A Moment occurred which allowed
An Experience to be recorded.

And through each Journey made,
A Chapter.

That Chapter kept going until the sequence
was broken.

Someone felt that creating Moments without Choices
felt right.

Moreover, doing things neutrally felt appropriate.

Therefore, the Book ends Uncompleted.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
