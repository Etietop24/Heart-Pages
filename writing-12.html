WHISPER 12 · FANTASY REFLECTION

Enchanted by a Mere Mortal

28.08.2026 4 min read

Had it all been a lie? Enveloped with courage and a sound mind, it was never a joke to make friends. It had always been a delightful atmosphere, having conversations with people of different races and calibers.

Despite it all, we got into negotiation, and though all was going on a smooth path, we never suspected tragedy on the path. Along the line, we witnessed a series of calamities and moments of weakness—times when we had to lay down our glamour, not because of the magic surrounding us, but because of our mortal mindset.

We got carried away by the glittering craft offered by the mortal being we were possessed with. Despite the enchantments—or, sorry, the craft used to deceive us—we have been made to detect the evil conspiracy in the eye of the deceiver.

Looking discreetly, we never can tell all the evil strategies planned by their alluring mind. We have fallen under their spell; we have succumbed to their flimsy excuses as they manipulated us into doing their bidding.

Breaking our Good Law had come to mind as we launched into the world with them. We got warning signs, red lights, and education about the numerous tricks they could play on us, and the different encounters that have destroyed so many lives just by adhering to their audacious tongue.

All covered with glamour, we played along. But now that our craft has awakened to revive the blast of victory over the so-called Fair Folk, we stepped into the battlefield with our unknown craft—mysterious to the world and immune to every enchantment by their glamour.

We looked and smiled at the face that shone across their countenance, a face that showed the opposite of success. A face a mortal could understand. A face tamed by a mere Mortal. A face that lasts and shines in the darkness of an Autumn Courtland.

“A face tamed by a mere Mortal.”
