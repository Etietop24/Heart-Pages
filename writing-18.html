WHISPER 18 · FRAGMENT

What's the Catch?

27.09.2026 4 min read

The fact that he knew was what hurt the most.

I was someone in charge of my feelings. I have had goals that kept me at the top of my game. The way he somehow made me realize that I wasn’t yet the best was what put me in distress.

Down to the outfits I wore, I realized that I became a lot self-conscious about how I look, the way I dressed.

The awareness he did create around led me to consider many things. I knew I wasn’t a creative person when it came to the outside world but for him, I began to create clips and shorts that showed a part of me I never knew existed.

He was the kind of guy you would consider to be dangerous and capable of being a player but the more he caressed my neck, the more I fell under his spell.

He said it as if he knew I was the one who somehow needed it. When he spoke to other girls, it made me jealous. To be honest, I wanted a situation that only I was eligible to be his friend or his right hand girl.

At the beginning, it was just a quest for competition; who did it better? Over time, it became as if we had been blood related. People started to get the impression that we do know each other. Yes, I loved the attention but I didn’t want a notion of us dating or doing what people that are older did if you get what I mean.

Now, it felt like I was expecting a conversation, a touch, or the vibe that he still realizes my prescence in the room. Everyone also knew that I get what I want if I want it. It was like a mantra that followed me for a while. And of course, I did know my worth so having to compete with something that proved higher felt like a very frustrating challenge.

Earlier, I had thoughts that I had it under control but when I encountered this guy on the second contact, it felt like I actually just met my match.

Moreover, each time, I proved overlord I also still felt small.

So now the catch is get better at this thing than him but still be in your right state of mind.

However, there is a problem

How do you concentrate on your main goal knowing fully that your competition is a dangerous game?

When I say dangerous, I mean a sore eye for beauty.

Moreover, one funny part is that he knows that he is a beauty.

How do you secure your target without falling into their trap?

Many call it ‘The Strategic Intimacy’

Yeah, it is basic because your rival makes you falls for them without you knowing their deadly plan.

I could say I am used to most of those despicable threats but I just want to know, what’s the catch?

For a job?

A company, a big gig?

Am I another name on your played list?

I wanna know,

Mr. Royal, is the thing we are about to enter a course for you to stay afloat or does this make any sense to you?

In honest words, am I a joke to you?

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
