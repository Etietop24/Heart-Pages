Why Me

19.08.2026 4 min read

Did we really have to meet each other at the library?

I definitely didn’t have plans for a situationship or a relationship. I thought I could walk through this part of college without necessarily getting involved.

I mean, my past relationships had been a flop. I decided on my own that it wouldn’t repeat itself.

Well, to be frank, I rather misread the fact that things could explode. Even though I was always ignorant, I did know that K-drama had scenes like this.

Ignorantly, I had to make friends and you happened to be right there beside me. I said, “What could eventually happen?”

Numerous conversations, humorous expressions were all we did while I focused on taking my grades to the next level.

The experience definitely was a thrilling one as only we could hang out. I didn’t even realize my best friend dying down the drain until I eventually had to cut to the chase.

I did what I had to do because I wasn’t actually being a good friend. It didn’t actually affect me because there was a YOU.

After a while, I realized there was something more to this than just a YOU, and I decided, let’s make it rest.

However, nature just said, “Why not have a Prince Charming for a Bride?”

Although I didn’t think of it that way until we finally reached here.

When I still come up with the question, “Why Me?” it’s actually an unanswerable question because I don’t understand what is in me that makes YOU wanna cleave.

And what makes me wanna crave more of this intimacy? I definitely will remove that, but I guess it is for the best.

However, for whatever reason, I am still in awe of how we started this journey, so I’m still like:

“How did we go from chilling and cruising to falling head over heels for each other, carrying your words in my head and making your every move a dance I’m gonna learn?”

Nevertheless, even though YOU changed me and I still love YOU, I guess I gotta lose YOU on the way to finding me!

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
