Stripped Bare by Him

18.08.2026 5 min read

How did it all start? When am asked how I transited from the timid and hateful being I was to the free-spirited and freelancer that I had turned into.

People ask me the same question. As I cast my mind backward, I tend to laugh and wonder how much of difference had occurred. It has been a wonder hating people and then getting the love I now have from the same set.

I had encountered a Folk on my usual tour in the cold and withered garden I had called my life. I had led the most wretched and there, no friends encamped. I was strolling casually when I noticed a light emitting from a hut close by; with much interest, I got concerned.

Although, I decided to let it lie, as it seemed of less importance but by each day closer, the energy I felt with the mystery creature I had not laid eyes on continued to expand and deepen. I felt the power of the cold mindset begin to shiver as the hot flaming vibe passed through my garden.

Each day, I drew nearer to the meeting where I will order him out of the garden as a nuisance I called him. Knock went my heart and I was agitated to listen to a knock but reluctantly, I opened and there and then was the creature I had not believed could make me bare.

From that encounter, I felt stripped to the bone, his presence made me empty, made me known to my crazy attitude. It informed me of a life I needed to taste, a world I could taste in him.

He held me close but cause of the discomfort, I withdrew as I felt inferior to his glamour, in his eyes, I felt ruined but he reassured me of a perfect life.

There and then, a part told me that spring and summer are bound to appear in a while time. I laughed it off as the winter in my heart was enough to cover the earth but the constant smiles and laughter unbelievably caused the icy stone to break and the hot flaming energy I had perceived at a distance melted it down into a river.

With the winter gone at a go, a sunshine spark across the sky and the smile that got me fantastic praises busted out.

He rained me with things I had long ago forgotten about. My amazing dreams of incredible attributes all came back at full force as a power ranger exploiting its power on the enemy.

He enlightened me that my smile had the earth healed, he explained that am crafted with the best personality and showed me the path to a colorful and beautiful garden where I dreamt no more but all in reality.

He kept in constant thought of what I look like. He kept the light shining, never leaving the day without my smile.

With how the wind had turned me into, I flowed with the evil vibe and became a rotten grandma but since he captured my heart, spoiled me with self-confidence and increased the daring lioness.

Ever since then, I vowed that until I meet him again, this would be my forever plea:

My Heart, will you leave without a word and will you keep me in suspense as I await your return? Can you make this period less unbearable?

While you are at that, can you return what you stole from me, My Pride?

Taking my Pride had caused me to live a Lawless Life.
