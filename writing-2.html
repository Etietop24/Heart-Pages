How Do I Go From Here?

17.08.2026 3 min read

You know you did break me into two…

Let’s not start all hateful. We made each other friends, we spoke the language of friends or, not to say best friends, we were always together though not in the same class.

We did make sure that distance was not a barrier. I was hard, tough and thick but you came and melted me, you dissolved the hardcore girl that lived within. You spoke the right words, it seemed that you were next to being perfect and for that, I fell deeper into the idea that I wasn’t jinxed.

You removed inferiority complex of hatred, you opened up my little world and made it a lot brighter, enlightened me the more about people’s peculiarities.

You taught me a lot about how nothing comes out of apologizing. Truly, you were always what I had wanted. You obviously heard countless that I was depressed. With my crazy and overbearing attitude, you lasted a whole month with me, made every day a lot special.

Gave me another hope at being close to people, not cause they will act but they can be real. I believed your every closeness, your touch, your words, your smile and how you always said that I was important.

I ejected my careless thoughts of losing you because I promised to make things work out no matter wat. Yeah, I did hate clinginess but I could feel the vibe of friendship as reality. I learned to smile with my heart not my teeth, spoke with my mind more often.

I told others that you were one of the reason I got happier each time, why I decided to associate more. You became the first I could express myself with. I thought I would never get the chance again but your sincerity made it real.

Sincerely, there is one huge problem.

You broke me, moved my heart into pieces, shattered them around and left me to pick them up within three weeks.

How do I go from here, My Gem?
