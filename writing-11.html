WHISPER 11 · REFLECTION

The Bridge

26.08.2026 4 min read

As I lay there, the only thing I had in mind was completing this next book, The Bridge.

It wasn't just another book.

It was an answer. It wasn't another hundred-and-something pages filled with more chapters.

It was a clue, a direction, and an open door.

Each of us has had our downtime.

For Charlie, it was his father's opinion about trading with cement.

For Donna, it was her family—the constant reminder that what gave way to their demise was drugs.

For Molly, it was the fact that her father got to decide what she did.

For Alex, it was something.

Lastly, for Edna, it was Tom's death.

But when we look at every character, they all hold one thing in common:

The Bridge.

They had a second chance.

Charlie and Donna had many children through the Bridge.

Edna had her heart healed through a book at the Bridge.

Allen and Molly made it work because of something they found at the Bridge.

“What exactly is the Bridge?”

It was a pathway for people who wanted to leave the past.

It was a step toward a brighter pathway.

It was a crossing from mediocrity to magnification.

It was a movement of hope—from the tears and pain of the past to the fulfillment of tomorrow.

It was a choice for a second chance.

It was a time to reflect and review the hidden truths underneath the false lies you had heard.

It was a junction where two lives were restored, reunited, and knitted together.

It was a hope for tomorrow.

It was the feeling of waking and being able to look into a better future.

For you, it could be a second chance.

It could be the point where you realize that you have been doing it all wrong.

It could be where you need a cup of coffee at someone's desk or bedside.

It could be a dinnertime where someone finally hears you.

It could be that moment when darkness stops closing in around you.

It could be the moment to take your time and observe, listen, and breathe.

It could be asking God where He was.

It could be recalling God's faithfulness.

It could be a moment when the end has actually come.

“It is moving up from the yesterday of pain, regret to the possibility of tomorrow.”

So, let's say you have faced a lot of crises, pain, and shame, of course. But when the point where the Bridge comes into the picture, a lot of chances they call miracles surely happen.

Please hold on to God, and don't let today's pain and tears stop you from believing in the God who brought you this far.

When He wants to bridge you back to normality, even you won't be able to doubt that, one way or the other, you have moved from the shackles of the past to the beauty of tomorrow.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
