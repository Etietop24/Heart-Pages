WHISPER 07

A Clue of You

August 21, 2026

Never have I felt this touch before. The thought keeps countering my actions.

I thought I was alone. I knew the world had all left me. It wasn’t something debatable.

We doubted the amazing personality we got familiarized with because of the horrible past we had just left. The numerous souls we left behind were pains we faced as we commenced this new life.

Imagining, out of the blue, someone least expected turned out to lurk behind as we moved on through the earth. Oftentimes, we see this certain creature in our dreams, hoping to find a definite reason—but never do.

Despite our wrong ways towards such, we are kept in awe at how a part of us still yearns for a closeness. We emerged with high hopes as we forsook the past and looked forward to a new life, but still got hit back with a clue of only you.

Man, what’s in you that drives us crazy? Just wanna know.

We often realize from recent times how people don’t like the idea of being looked at as fools, but to us, it feels like an accomplishment as we play hide and seek together all day.

We ought to forget all that encompasses our past, but forgetting a beautiful gem has caused us to think twice—not the normal gems we see lurking around. Not a common one.

Someone we call glamorous, because your beauty is something out of this world. And yes, can you believe you make me think a dozen times about my decisions?

We could wake up and be like our purpose on this earth was to make you happy. We see you, and how much we crave you overwhelms us even more.

You know those things about forgetting something after a long time. Really, that might be all we need to escape this commanding vibe that causes motivations of different kinds to make the things we now find ourselves doing feel sweet.

It’s so much to contain—this intrinsic energy that continuously inspires. Your words are enough messages to feed on, and your manner of movement is a match for the missing pieces in the puzzle of my unsettled personality.

Though I love myself to bits, I can’t beat the fact that I crave your presence constantly.

So all I have been saying all along is, regardless of those past wasted months and time that has been lost, it was just a medium to prepare us both for the epic journey ahead.

I know all this might be too soon, but I doubt you wanna miss the amazing adventure—the world I’m looking at prepares for us.

If we could just take a chance to reflect on the past we have forsaken and understand that the present is beautiful and the future, a perfect bliss.

All I think about each day is how to make things right with you. It may never be the same, but it could lead somewhere—a place we could cherish for the rest of our lives.

Never have I ever felt this touch toward someone, someone I am yet to truly discover. A mysterious person. A beautiful soul who has somehow turned me into someone curious.

I have searched far and wide but couldn’t come to the knowledge of why her.

All my heart keeps pointing out the fact that she is truly someone you would definitely be thankful for.

So, to my craving, I wish we could have been better all along. But the height God is taking us to, I can’t see yet. Still, things we have never come to realize are starting right now.

I know you know this, but I think saying it again could make it a little less hard:

I do miss you from the bottom of my heart, where the craziest emotions lie.
