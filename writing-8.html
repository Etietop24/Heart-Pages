WHISPER 08 · LETTER

I’ll Be Waiting

25.08.2026 4 min read

You are crazy. Maybe it is all of us that are.

Meeting you has, at least, been one of the most amazing moments of my life.

I remember college, when I first got admitted as a freshman. I knew next to nothing. I also remember Fresher’s Night, where even those of us who knew nothing were acting all friendly. And I, of course, was seated out back with my AirPods. That night, all I could think about was how to scale this thing they called education and, I guess, establish myself.

That reminds me of how we started talking. You came up with the lamest excuse about buying me a drink. I guess if you weren't at least as fine as I thought you were, I wouldn't have considered it.

Then came the moments of actually having fun, like playing hockey during P.E. class. That's when I noticed something:

“Where were all my emotions in all of this?”

I remember how we talked about my nonchalance toward sports, and you pushed me into it. You dragged me into the school gym and helped me set up a schedule, even when I thought my grades would drop.

Despite all the girls who drooled over you, all the gossip, and all the sweet talks I heard about you in the dorm, you chose me.

I carried broken pieces, shattered behaviours, loopholes, and figures that had lost their shape. All of them somehow found their way into me.

Usually, I was the one with the worst rule of life: a guy just had to be sexy, and I'd fall hard. It became something close to a perfect addiction.

I was called names. Even the guys who had their fun with me made me feel like I was worth nothing when they insulted me. It left me drowning in doubt, and eventually, I started believing that I was wasted.

Every day, I tried to wash away the guilt and shame of everything I had done. I cried. I begged. I pushed through. But every time I asked myself, “Why?” the answer seemed to be the same:

My past was my label.

The past I saw was one where my Mom gave birth to me as a whore. It kept reminding me that no matter where I went, I couldn't escape it. I felt as though even heaven couldn't look upon me with mercy.

I couldn't sit still with the chaos in my mind. I thought about opening up to my friends, Clara and Kavin, but I was too scared of their reactions. So I kept patterns and things I had been told to cover up hidden away.

When I finally sought counsel, I learned that the only way forward was to open up, let the truth be seen, and fight through it.

That meant exposing myself.

But how?

I thought the whole world would know. I thought I would lose my mind if they did.

But I guess it was relieving when I finally told you.

Maybe it really is true that a problem shared is half solved.

You made it easier. You held me close, knowing fully well what the problem was. Although you had your own things to deal with, you still believed that I could survive.

And yeah, I'm sorry about that thing that happened.

But through all these commotions, why did you bring life and love into mine, only to break my heart afterward?

Was all of this a joke?

Did I really not mean anything?

I guess it was all a joke after all.

But I hope you realize that you are still a somebody.

For the time being—

Get your shit together.

✦

I will be waiting.

— HER
