Letter to My Summer Fling

18.10.2026 · 3 min read

As I glance back, I wonder where it all began from. It was always never gonna be a harmful conversation.

It never really involved both parties feeling like they should coexist in the same space.

So, if am being honest with you and everyone I have come to know. I would say that you read this just when you wanna know if I eventually got obsessed.

Dear Royal,

This is probably a day after I left our little date place, where our feelings grew for no reason. Yes, if I am being honest with everyone.

I did become obsessed with you not because you were a pretty shit but because you commanded words and feelings that had been buried

I always looked for a moment with you, not that I wanted to do midnight calls with you.

I craved each day much more as it ended, I wanted your words to be reality.

I thought of how you somehow made me blush despite how dangerous you were.

We did talk about the common parts of life but every time I remember that you cannot be mine, my heart broke in a million pieces.

Even then I tried to prove that I was more worth your feelings, I tried all my possible charisma but to no avail.

So, if am being honest now, it was never the feelings that caught me on you, it was never cause you spoke to me consistently or the fact that you made my day almost amazing.

I also thought it was about the lunch date we had in the common room or the fact that we walk home together cause even at that I always brought a conversation.

It was the fact that my mind couldn’t shake off the thoughts of you out of my life

Trust me, it was that my mind couldn’t stand another challenge

It was also that I felt things that I have kept locked away from the world

It was also that the thoughts of the touch you gave brought things that erupted my inside,

They were like fire that tore my insides apart,

I never believed that from the mere thought of a challenge could turn me into something someone you love

I became your constant at break and on the drive home, it felt like it was for us alone

So, yes,

Love was never invited

It was never welcomed

It wasn’t even included into our schedule

But despite our cautious conversations and interactions, love managed to creep in

Yeah, it wasn’t our fault

I could blame me for the initiation but I rather let it slide cause we could have stop things when they had no roots but someone had to let it go on.

Now, if I was confronted by love, if it asked,

“Did you want to go a journey where the one you love is attracted to another? “…and will you fight for him or let him be?”

I stepped back and looked back from where am coming from, how I had declined other shots and now something like perfect came.

I see how the touch and contact has made me feel, how I had suddenly allowed my walls, my rules, my conviction melt away just with feel of his hands.

I realize that it could be okay for a short while but that kind thing they call Summer Fling

I smiled at Love and said,

“could have wanted it but I will pass.”

It wasn’t worth losing my conviction over.

Slowly, Kamin played in the background as if saying ‘its time to leave’

I guess the answer I gave Love is the same I give you, Royal.

It was lovely fighting by your side, I hope we don’t become partners again in life’s game.

I will see you on my next flight to Kenya.

Love,
Lil Ms.

I guess I have to end the chapter faster than expected. Maybe when I am more ready to start something.

For now, I love you from the surface of my heart.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
