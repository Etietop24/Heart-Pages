THE UNFINISHED PAGE

Some thoughts never find their ending.

Not every thought becomes a complete story. Some stop halfway through a sentence, somewhere between feeling and saying.

This is where those words remain.

UNFINISHED · 01

I made a promise and one

I promised not to break

I tried cooperating but

Each time I failed

I gave up complaining

So I let it go

I opened up my heart

To every risk and challenges

Cause if you don't risk it…

I cried, wept and swore

I begged, laughed, smiled and joked

I also lied occasionally and of course

I cursed when it was too much

I prayed and pleaded for help

And I wished it will all go away

Sometimes I cared less and other times

I worried louder

I blushed when things were good

And I wailed when I wanted to

I spoke when it killed the gut in me

And I gave up the me that cared more

UNFINISHED · 02

Smile and it gets you flowers

Roses are red, Violet are blue

When the guy goes on his knees

But friendship is always the initial stage

A trip to the Bahamas makes it a date night

A knock on my door tells me its time for prom

A kiss or two makes it official

From chasing sunsets to cruising under the blanket

Catching stars in the sky waiting for a wish of ours

Could only be what we wish but never what we dreamed

As the years passed and it became old, I realized it all started with

Roses on my front porch, friendship at school, a few dates and making up after fights

I thought we had all the time in the world but you took us the point where we said Goodbye.
