The Inside or Outside Show

20.11.2026

I left my comfort zone just to be something I had never been and someone I had never been. I dressed up differently as I was going for party of course I had to look the part. Even though, I didn’t have makeup to be like them, I still wore the gown and the heels to cover up from the part I couldn’t fix.

It was a supposed one-time thing where I do what I had never done before. Moreover, it was supposed to be between God and I because him alone understood the weakness I have been dealing with.

In the chaos of a party, I had to come to terms that I had actually sneaked out to attend a party at midnight and to think that me the one person no one could imagine to do it. This time, it wasn’t curiosity or to satisfy my cravings but

“I wanted what I know I cannot have.”

That was the language.

And right there in my thoughts, I sparked an interest. Now, it was always the “Tall, Light skinned, glasses guy” that did it for me but that phase, it turned out to be a “Short, Dark-skinned, Handsome man”. Believe me; it wasn’t in me anymore to turn up the roles of playing gentle woman. It felt more like the story was clicking into its sequence.

Fast forward, I didn’t want a second time to be a different ME in quote. It was a one-time thing right?

“What I had planned to stay closed, just found its way out.”

Yea, I believed so much in risking, at this moment, I was scared to believe in it.

That one party took three weeks to suffice; it came again with a Number I guess. Remember, it was a one-time thing. And I did promise myself that consequences of such party will come and I will have to be accountable for it. So, I countered it when the numbers came. Again, Presence came. We met at unpredictable places like the Church; remember I was a Church Girl.

I told myself to forget it; it was Church. More encounters told me the Number won’t hurt and as I let the number in, I guess I saw the roses as it did spring around not to forget the sharp thorns I experienced. Not long after did I remember who I was before the party or the part of me that still existed somewhere deeply rooted, the Goodytooshoes, the one I silenced that night.

She spoke to me about the party and the risk involved. She had definitely witnessed a lot of Numbers and Presences but didn’t know that it could ever become a Contact.

Gradually, that Contact became a Thought, it was not bold, it was basic and simple nothing deep just enough to know it existed. In this Thought was the man I prayed against and I. Yea that was she before the party. It was part of the plan, do this thing and forget about everything you have a problem with. One way or the other, you would go off the radar.

Therefore, I prayed against HIM. The story gets interesting as the thought busted into a Feeling, a mild one at least. Now, both the man I prayed against and I are fully aware of certain emotions, which are unknown. Therefore, I went back to God, I prayed against the same man. I told him about strolls, the meetings and of course the conversation, my thoughts as well. Since, it kept coming; I stiffed but still placed my heels on his couch.

Guess at the beginning, the plan was once. Now where the whole world is watching at the Connection. People’s mind are “There is something there”. The producers are more like “Should we spice things up or allow them to play their course?” Close friends are grinning, “They ship this”.

Back to the couples or actors, the lady, knowing her background and conviction who decided to have fun once has clearly gotten into something she definitely not prepared while on the other hand, the man is also doing the whole touch thing and still knowing what he’s doing. Either ways, both parties are still trying to knock their head around something.

“One is holding on to Beliefs, the other Desires and Life.”

My question,

“How does the story end?”

By the way, I am Cassia, your host and here with me is Mystique, our compromising woman and Raven, she prays against. Whom are you rooting for?
