WHISPER 10 · REFLECTION

Dear Woman

26.08.2026 4 min read

I write as a woman.

Not because I have a womb, or simply because my gender is female, but because of the hardships that we, as women, face.

I have stumbled upon a few cases of women being threatened because of what is perceived as our ineptness. To be honest, it isn't our fault that our feelings are so easy to form. Besides, it isn't our fault that we can easily fall for a man's charm.

And because of these things, we are taken advantage of.

We are subjected to a life we begin to believe cannot be better without them. And so, we start to believe that we cannot live without the strong hands of our puppeteers.

We wonder why men cannot forgive us when we cannot give them what they want. They call us fools for being unable to win even the smallest battle. In their defence, it raises them higher and gives them as much power as they command.

To them, we are dependent on them.

Without them, we cannot survive.

Slowly, our minds begin to deteriorate. Our worth, our charisma, the very things that make us stand out, begin to lose their value.

I know a couple of people who are suffering out there because they weren't the best to their husbands, their boyfriends, or even to the people they never imagined would come to hate them.

They get beaten because they couldn't wear the same fancy clothes their Gen Z peers wore or speak the same language that goes around.

Is it the hot gossip they don't know about?

When they are confronted by their peers, they either shrink themselves in order to fit into someone else's shoes.

But my dear, behind the makeup, the couture dresses, the high fashion, behind closed doors—what exactly is that thing that makes you so scared of coming out?

Is it that the man you think loves you will hurt you?

Is it the video of your ineptness?

Was it the past?

Was it that the life you lived in the past taught you that you could do anything just to never return to it?

And for such reasons, have we made the wrong choices?

Have you died in silence?

Swallowed your pride?

Allowed another person to define your worth?

Your self-confidence?

The reason you were made?

“As a woman, I wrote.”
“Now, as a girl, I write.”

What is that thing that has kept your inner glow locked away?

What has made the sunshine in you grow dim?

Of course, it doesn't always have to be guys.

I just want to know what you would say made you who you are.

Would it be the fame you are chasing?

The man who has money and connections?

The fake lifestyle you want to live?

Or would you rather live in your own shoes and be the sunshine in that dark garden?

Just think about it.

Dear Woman.
