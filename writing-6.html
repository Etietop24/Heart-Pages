WHISPER 06 · DECLARATION

Ordered Request

20.08.2026 4 min read

Days have specific desires and meanings. A couple of some are ordinary, some distinct, others magnificent and the rest a mysterious vibe. Starting a casual day is not different from every other day as it is the same routine.

To obtain something, we got ask right. Sure, but to need something desperately, we were not just ask; we need to taste for it. Someone once said, “We got taste whatever sweet food we want to consume”. We have encountered many delicious humans and we were acquainted with their behaviors.

We have had time to mark them based on the different emotions that surrounds them when they come close. Our Perfection, Our Special Cocktail, “Her Priority”, “Her Curiosity”, we made people more important that we had to edit the names they are called ’cause according to the wolf in us, they need a soothing name.

In books, we are reminded of how our beloved, “The man, my man who has Love as a Name” had captured the part of our heart, we had considered irrelevant and of less significance.

We had objected traces of a single flint but still we got someone who could wait for seven damn years just to get us back together.

We had trailed away from the Normal rules of life and we been likened to Rebels when dealing with life. Even life had given up on us; we had resigned our fate to collecting from people who have precious gems each time sapping what is rightful theirs and still get away with it because of the influence, power and beauty we been blessed with.

We doubted our own benefits as a person in this world because we had gone opposite direction of what we had worked for. Even after finding a way out, since new to the system, we had numerous and gargantuan ways of backing out when a problem or something we are oblivious to appears.

Still we do not believe the opportunity the Spiritual Father had blessed us with. We frustrated the act of ever having hope ’cause we have destroyed the core balance of the game player.

Although all that, an Angel was born, right into our heart, someone pure and tender, a damsel, clean and beautiful.

When we desire to have our way, we get disturbed of how much ruin we can accomplish. True, we fell head heels but we kept our distance despite the innate forwardness we were born with, a spirit to command, order and of authority.

Moreover, when we want something, we just have to get it.

This is not to scare the ones we have picked, it is to make you all known to the personality we merged with. That despite all the clamour of being an Intimidating, freelancer and bitch that does not care one shit about what the world says about us.

As long as you are connected to my heart and the beat of your heart rhymes to the sound that plays in my ear or the blood that correlates with the veins in my body.

Even though, we speak different behavioral lives and our paths are diswayed from one another.

As far as my Wolf gets to howl your name, you are one person who is Marked by a Royalty and you are Mine, so no matter what happens, I hope the answer I get now will also get to be the only words I hear:

“Am the person, your Heart will tell you: Am your Forever Vow”

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
