Was It Self-Love or…

26.08.2026 4 min read

I always thought life with people was amazing. I thought about life and the way it looked through the eyes of others, and how, somehow, I had turned out differently.

I kept so many of them close. I met the ones I thought I could spend the rest of my life with. They were like the Perfect Find.

To be honest, every time I tried to create a scenario that would keep them at the doorstep of my heart, they turned on their heels and walked away. Then life went on as if they had never existed.

And sometimes, I felt like doing it first—running off before they could leave me. I had to ask myself whether I was malfunctioning or whether whatever it took to have friends simply wasn't in me.

I have considered a lot because of this. I have had therapy to escape the lingering spirit of low self-esteem. I've had to ask questions that left people wondering.

Amid all the confidence I displayed on the outside, deep within was a timid, scared, and intimidated girl. I've met counsellors who talked me out of getting worked up over things I couldn't control.

Guys come and go. The ones I had gotten involved with always seemed to want a relationship or something that wasn't worth giving myself away for.

Now the question is:

“Would I rather go on a journey of not choosing love?”

To answer fairly, yes—if it means choosing myself.

I wouldn't choose a life where my self-worth depends on someone else, or where a man gets to define my next genuine move or my ability to be loved.

You know, many of us indulge in things we aren't proud of. We change ourselves because we feel like we didn't do something right.

To be honest, it's a shitty feeling when you go ahead and make that decision for yourself. You shrink yourself just so one person can look at you with affection before you finally believe you're worthy of love.

Because, just so you know, when you start asking and demanding for something that is rightfully yours, maybe it was never yours from the start.

Love isn't something you beg for.

It isn't something with a time limit.

It certainly isn't something you pay for in a market.

Dammit, you don't put it up for auction.

If you lack love, start with the love you give yourself.

If that doesn't exist, then you will keep searching for it somewhere else.

Trust me, you will sweat before you find it.

Not everyone wearing a smile is ready to give one to someone who has been deprived of it.

I don't have the words to describe my own battle with feeling like I wasn't enough, but I can tell the story of finding love in the small things I do for myself.

I learnt to be enough for me.

That alone was enough to help me find love.

Not because it drew guys to me.

Not because what I had wanted for so long finally came.

But because, nevertheless, no one could talk me down.

No. Not even from believing in the very best person in my life.

Me.

Therefore, if I'm supposed to choose again—

A hundred times,

it would still be me.

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
