ABOUT

For the thoughts that never found a voice.

Whispers Within was created for the things we sometimes cannot say aloud—the feelings that remain somewhere between thought and silence.

I write what I feel, what I have experienced, and what has stayed with me long after the moment has passed.

Perhaps somewhere in these words, you might find something that sounds a little like you.

Because I Love You Sometimes I feel depressed, not because you hurt me, but because I love you more deeply than I know how to handle. I care so much that your absence feels heavy, your silence feels loud, and even the smallest distance between us can hurt. I wish loving you could always feel beautiful, but sometimes it scares me how much of my heart I’ve given to you. I just hope you understand that beneath all my silence is someone who simply loves you deeply and doesn’t want to lose you.
