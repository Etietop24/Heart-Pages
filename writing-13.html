A Thousand Miles

29.08.2026 4 min read
“Could we actually call that the first thousand miles?”

A journey of a thousand miles begins with the first step.

That's all they say to us. It's what we hear when we start.

We were never told about the deep thinking or the confused mood swings. They didn't tell us how we would make choices. Neither did they give us a heads-up about it.

The concept was a mystery, a puzzle to be solved through thorough investigation.

Still—“a journey… begins with the first step.”

The first step that could determine the ones that follow.

Steps like: what's going to happen when we lie to one another?

Another like keeping secrets away from each other.

When you have to explain something you think she won't understand.

When you can't understand why you said yes.

When you were supposed to love the second, not the first, anymore.

When you have every right to ditch, backstab, but you didn't.

Or paint an empty wall with your worst fear.

In the name of graffiti, you showed the world that nothing should come in between.

When your craziest reason can't be understood.

And you have every right to compromise, to seek payback, but you didn't.

And you are called by that spirit to do your worst.

But you had enjoyed a thousand days—not yet a thousand miles.

You spent a thousand weeks, not close to a thousand miles.

You chased a thousand months, and it's still not up to the miles with three zeroes.

What about the thousand years that had been linked with unforgettable memories?

Could we actually call that the first thousand miles?

A thousand days—or more days than now.

A thousand weeks—or more hours to all this.

A thousand months from now—or a time of double 31 days.

A thousand years—or a memory box.

“Which could you do—a decision of a lifetime.”

✦

For those with unspoken thoughts, may you find a little peace here.
