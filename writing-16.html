When You Think You Love Her…

20.09.2026 · 4 min read

“You got to understand that I don’t love you”.

There is a lot meaning from that sentence. I had to make a decision that’s a negative.

Making close companions is not hard to come by but when you make friends with the mind that you really want them to know you, it does mean many things.

We got acquainted and had nothing in common, we doped as teenagers just cruising in the earth but we still thought we were humans actually adults.

We had zeroed our thoughts on making a relationship but a relationship is bound to come along. We exchanged numbers but it did seem like journey of no return at the first. Yeah I do love it that way.

The way you always wanted to talk to me or steal a glance, touch my palm all gets me confused. We don’t have to listen to the one hour sermon of someone’s feelings, that’s cliché.

I understand we possess a certain aura that grants us deep access to exclusive attention and respect. Believe me; you don’t know how wide your dreams are.

Actually, could we make things clear? You want us; you have to get us by force. There are things you have to know. Are you willing to believe and trust us?

Are you ready to stay and make us loved?

Are you humble to tell our parents that you can go crazy for us?

If you were to lose your ex for us, will you think twice?

We are filled with the craziest behavior; you sure don’t wanna become a laughing stock?

Assurance sometimes we lack, will you give us when we need it?

I am not trying to get to dislike us.

Will you be okay if you have to be friends with an anti-social freak?

Trust me not every girl who smiled the brightest smile has the prettiest problems. You know a lot charm and small talks on phone is not enough to trust you when you say you love her.

Compliments are not hard for guys to do but you know they do look themselves in the mirror. Let me tell you something, if you find a girl like her, you will never believe she did exist.

One vital information, be careful of your approach, not all the fanciful brides are willing to comprehend and contend with a gentleman like yourself.

I believe once more that love is supposed to be heck serious and like they says, it loves Flaws and All. It eliminates perfections, it doesn’t always have straight paths but still doesn’t hold the word CURVY as a pillar.

I wonder how you gotta say after dreaming that your precious little babe actually doesn’t appreciate the huge action; Love.

Of course, there’s a solution to this dilemma.

Don’t be the one to hold the hand, don’t be one to close the door, don’t be the one who holds it back, don’t boast that she has it but…
Know that you are the one to hold her heart, the one to open her doors and the one to let it all out when its too much.

Just make her know that she GOT it all.
